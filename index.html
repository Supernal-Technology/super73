Supernal × Super73 · Customer Service AI Operating System

AI-Native Customer Support

One funnel. One AI layer. Phone-first.

For a brand whose community is its growth engine, support is where loyalty is won or lost. We unify a fragmented stack and put one AI Employee at the front door: it answers the phone, resolves the everyday cases, and hands the rest to the right team with full context.

North star
From four fragmented intake points to one unified funnel, fronted by a single AI Employee.
Business case
Every repeated question is a failure of the stack. Every context-less transfer is a failure of the handoff.
What Super73 already has
Zendesk · helpdesk & system of record Mavenoid · hardware troubleshooting Narvar · order tracking & returns Knowledge base · docs, FAQs, manuals Four teams · Retail / Talent Pop / Dealer / Service
None of these is the problem; each does its job. The gap is that they don't share a customer, don't talk to each other, and leave the customer to guess which of four teams to call. A workflow problem, not a tooling one.
What we build on top
  • →First, one customer. Zendesk, Mavenoid, and Narvar unified into one profile, so the AI Employee knows who's calling and their full history from the first second.
  • →Then, one front door. It answers the phone, handles the everyday cases on its own, and warm-transfers the rest with full context.
  • →Nothing gets ripped out. Zendesk stays the system of record; the AI Employee orchestrates the existing tools, it doesn't replace them.
  • →Then we scale. In Phase 2 every interaction becomes signal: failure trends to engineering, automatic warranty, one analytics view across the stack.
What we heard
A retail customer can't be transferred to Talent Pop. Dealers call a separate line. Service issues route to yet another team.
Fragmentation cost
Four teams, three tools, no shared customer. Every handoff is a cold restart, and on the phone, the priciest channel there is, that cost compounds into repeats and wait time. Fixing the front door is the highest-leverage move in support.
CS sits on a goldmine of product signals that never makes it to the product team in any structured way.
Signal loss
Every call carries which part failed, on which model, at what mileage, exactly the signal the product team needs to catch issues early. Today it dies as ticket text, so failure trends stay invisible until they're already expensive.
Mavenoid, Narvar, and Zendesk each offer AI separately. We want one consistent flow with one analytics layer.
Orchestration gap
Each tool ships its own AI, but none sees the inbound call or talks to the others. The fix isn't a fourth tool, it's one brain on top that calls each through its API and reads Shopify, NetSuite, and Comodule. Owned by Super73, not rented per resolution.
